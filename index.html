ABOUT GEO ZINDAGI

Recovery Starts With the Right Support.

Geo Zindagi is a drug rehabilitation center located in Alkhalil Colony, Multan. Our goal is to help patients and their families explore recovery support in a respectful, private and welcoming environment.

We believe that each person's situation deserves individual attention. Contact our team to learn about available care, admission requirements and appropriate treatment options.

  • ✓ Information for patients and their families
  • ✓ Patient pickup arrangements on request
